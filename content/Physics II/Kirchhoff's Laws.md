# First Law

$$
\Sigma I=0
$$
In an intersection (A node where 3 or more wires intersect).

> [!note]-
> $I$ here is signed. Positive for incomming current, and negative for outgoing, or the inverse: as long as its consistant.

# Second Law

$$
\begin{align}
&\Sigma V=0 \\
&\oint_{\Gamma} E\cdot ds=0
\end{align}
$$
Around a closed loop, since electrostatic force is conservative.

## Faraday-Henry-Lenz Law

When there is a non-uniform, the integral form does not apply. We therefore must modify it to:

$$
\oint_{\Gamma}\vec{E}(\vec{r})\cdot d\vec{s}=- \frac{d\Phi_{B}}{dt}=\frac{W}{q}=\text{emf}
$$
Meaning that [[Physics II/Magnetostatics\|Magnetism]] and [[Physics II/Electrostatics\|Electric fields]] are linked.

[[Physics II/DC Generators#EMF\|EMF]] is the work done per unit charge by an electric field generated by a time-dependant magnetic flux.
