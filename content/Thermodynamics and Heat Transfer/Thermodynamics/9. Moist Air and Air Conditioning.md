*Prerequisites: [[2. Working Fluids#Gibb's Rule|Gibbs rule]], ideal gas mixtures*

## Ideal gas mixtures
Non-reacting ideal gases → mixture behaves ideally. **Gibbs:** $V=C-F+2$ with $C=N$ components, $F=1$ phase ⇒ $V=N+1$ (e.g. 3 gases: need 4 independent variables).

| Quantity | Definition |
|---|---|
| Moles / mass | $n_{mix}=\sum n_i$, $m_{mix}=\sum m_i$ |
| Mole fraction | $y_i=n_i/n_{mix}$ |
| Mass fraction | $f_{mi}=m_i/m_{mix}$ |
| Mean molar mass | $MM_{mix}=\sum y_i MM_i$ |
| Gas constant | $R_{mix}=\bar{R}/MM_{mix}$ |

**Dalton:** $p_{mix}=\sum p_i(T,V_{mix})$ ⇒ $y_i=p_i/p_{tot}$

**Amagat:** $V_{mix}=\sum V_i(T,p_{mix})$ ⇒ $y_i=V_i/V_{tot}$

$$
y_i=\frac{p_i}{p_{tot}}=\frac{V_i}{V_{tot}} \qquad f_{mi}=\frac{y_i MM_i}{MM_{mix}}
$$

- **Extensive** properties additive: $U_{mix}=\sum U_i$, $H_{mix}=\sum H_i$
- **Intensive** not additive: $u_{mix}=\sum f_{mi} u_i$

## Moist air
Mixture of **dry air** + **water vapor** (superheated). Dry air: $MM_{air}\approx 28.96\,\text{kg/kmol}$, $R_a=287\,\text{J/kgK}$. Water vapor: $R_v=461.5\,\text{J/kgK}$.

**Dalton:** $p=p_a+p_v$ ( $p_v\ll p_a$ typically).

### Key properties
**Specific humidity (humidity ratio):**

$$
\boxed{\omega=\frac{m_v}{m_a}=0.622\,\frac{p_v}{p-p_v}} \quad \left(\frac{\text{kg}_v}{\text{kg}_a}\right)
$$

**Relative humidity:**

$$
\boxed{\varphi=\frac{m_v}{m_s(T)}=\frac{p_v}{p_s(T)}\times 100\%} \qquad \omega=0.622\,\frac{\varphi\,p_s}{p-\varphi\,p_s}
$$

At fixed $\omega$ (same vapor mass), $\varphi$ still changes with temperature because the saturation pressure $p_s(T)$ changes.

**Specific enthalpy** (per kg dry air, $t$ in °C):

$$
h=h_a+\omega h_v \qquad h_a=c_{p,a}\,t \quad (c_{p,a}=1.006\,\text{kJ/kg}_a\text{K})
$$

$$
h_v=r_0+c_{p,v}\,t \quad (r_0=2500\,\text{kJ/kg},\,c_{p,v}=1.875\,\text{kJ/kgK})
$$

$$
\boxed{h=c_{p,a}\,t+\omega\,(r_0+c_{p,v}\,t)}
$$

Reference states: $h_a=0$ at $0\,^\circ\text{C}$; water vapor uses saturated liquid at $0\,^\circ\text{C}$ as zero and adds $r_0$. Enthalpy is per **kg dry air**, not per kg total moist air.

**Mass flow:** $\dot{m}_a=\dot{m}_{1+\omega}/(1+\omega)$. **Specific volume:** $v_a=R_a T/p_a$, $v_{1+\omega}\approx v_a$.

### Dew point
Cooling at **constant $p$** and constant composition first brings state 1 to the dew point $d$, where $p_v=p_s(T_d)$. Below $T_d$, vapor condenses: $m_v$ and $\omega$ decrease, and the remaining vapor is saturated with $p_v=p_s(T)$.

### Adiabatic saturation / wet bulb
Air passes over water, adiabatic, exits **saturated** ($\varphi_2=100\%$). Mass: $\dot{m}_f=\dot{m}_a(\omega_2-\omega_1)$. Energy ($h_f\ll h_1,h_2$): **$h_1\cong h_2$** — same enthalpy line on psychrometric chart.

Wet-bulb temperature is measured with a thermometer covered by wet gauze and ventilated by air flow; evaporation cools the bulb, so $h_{\text{dry bulb}}\approx h_{\text{wet bulb}}$.

## Balances (steady, no shaft work, $\Delta KE,\Delta PE \approx 0$)

**Mass:**
$$\dot{m}_{a,1}=\dot{m}_{a,2}=\dot{m}_a \qquad \dot{m}_w=\dot{m}_a(\omega_2-\omega_1)$$

**Energy:**
$$\dot{Q}_{CV}=\dot{m}_a(h_2-h_1)\pm \dot{m}_w h_w$$

**Adiabatic mixing** (two streams → 3):

$$
\frac{\dot{m}_{a,1}}{\dot{m}_{a,2}}=\frac{\omega_3-\omega_2}{\omega_1-\omega_3}=\frac{h_3-h_2}{h_1-h_3}
$$

### Common processes (Mollier / psychrometric chart)
| Process | Constraint | Heat |
|---|---|---|
| Sensible heating/cooling | $\omega=\text{const}$ | $\dot{Q}=\dot{m}_a(c_{p,a}+\omega c_{p,v})(t_2-t_1)$ |
| Dehumidification + cooling | Cool below $T_d$ | $\dot{m}_w=\dot{m}_a(\omega_1-\omega_2)$; $\dot{Q}\approx\dot{m}_a(h_2-h_1)<0$ |
| Humidification (vapor) | Adiabatic | slope $\Delta h/\Delta\omega=h_v$ |
| Humidification (liquid water) | Adiabatic | slope $\approx h_f$ → nearly **$h=\text{const}$** |

**Sensible vs latent split:** $\dot{Q}=\dot{Q}_{sens}+\dot{Q}_{lat}$ with $\dot{Q}_{sens}=\dot{m}_a(h_2-h_3)$, $\dot{Q}_{lat}=\dot{m}_a(h_3-h_1)$. Point 3 is the chart corner: one segment changes $t$ at constant $\omega$ (sensible), the other changes $\omega$ at constant $t$ (latent).

**Bypass factor** (actual coil): part of the dry-air flow bypasses the coil at state 1 while the rest reaches saturated state $S$; the exit state 2 is their mixture.

$$
f_{BP}=\frac{\dot{m}'_a}{\dot{m}''_a}=\frac{h_2-h_s}{h_1-h_2}
$$

## Air conditioning (comfort & loads)

**Comfort targets (indicative):**
- Winter: $t\approx 21\pm1\,°\text{C}$, $\varphi\approx 50\%\pm10\%$
- Summer: $t\approx 26\pm1\,°\text{C}$, $\varphi\approx 50\%\pm10\%$
- Injection velocity $\approx 0.15\,\text{m/s}$; **air renewal** $\dot{m}_{a,I}\geq \dot{m}_{a,r}$

**Loads:**
$$\dot{Q}_t=\dot{Q}_s+\dot{Q}_l \qquad \dot{Q}_s>0\text{ (summer)},\ \dot{Q}_s<0\text{ (winter, almost always)}$$

$$
\dot{Q}_s=\dot{Q}_{s,ex}+\dot{Q}_{s,p}+\dot{Q}_{s,d}+\dot{Q}_{s,inf}+\dot{Q}_{s,irr}
$$

$$
\dot{Q}_l=\sum_i \dot{m}_{v,i}h_v(t_E), \qquad \sum_i \dot{m}_{v,i}=n_p\dot{m}_{v,p}+\dot{m}_{v,d}+\dot{m}_{inf}(\omega_{out}-\omega_E)
$$

**Injected air state** (environment $E$, injection $I$, $\omega_E>\omega_I$ when dehumidifying):

$$
\dot{Q}_t=\dot{m}_a(h_E-h_I) \qquad \sum \dot{m}_{v,i}=\dot{m}_a(\omega_E-\omega_I)
$$

On psychrometric chart: line through $E$ with slope $\dot{Q}_t/\sum\dot{m}_{v,i}\approx h_E-h_I$ over $\omega_E-\omega_I$.

**Temperature lift limits:** summer $t_E-t_I\lesssim 8\text{–}12\,°\text{C}$; winter $t_I-t_E\lesssim 12\text{–}20\,°\text{C}$ (avoid tiny $\Delta t$ → huge $\dot{m}_a$).

### Winter scheme (heating + humidification)
$\dot{Q}_t/\sum\dot{m}_{v,i}<0$, $h_E<h_I$. Total heating power:

Mixing point with outdoor renewal air $R$:

$$
h_M=\frac{(\dot{m}_{a,I}-\dot{m}_{a,R})h_E+\dot{m}_{a,R}h_R}{\dot{m}_{a,I}}
$$

$$
\dot{Q}=\dot{Q}_t+\dot{m}_{a,R}(h_E-h_R)
$$

(preheat outdoor $R$, mix at $M$, humidify along/near saturation, post-heat to $I$).

### Summer scheme (cooling + dehumidification)
$\dot{Q}_t/\sum\dot{m}_{v,i}>0$, $h_E>h_I$:

Use the same mixing point:

$$
h_M=\frac{(\dot{m}_{a,I}-\dot{m}_{a,R})h_E+\dot{m}_{a,R}h_R}{\dot{m}_{a,I}}
$$

$$
\dot{Q}_{refr}=\dot{m}_{a,I}(h_1-h_M)<0 \qquad \dot{Q}_t=\dot{Q}_{refr}+\dot{m}_{a,I}(h_I-h_1)
$$

Equivalent expanded form:

$$
\dot{Q}_{refr}=\dot{m}_{a,I}(h_1-h_E)+\dot{m}_{a,R}(h_E-h_R)<0
$$

Cooling coil to saturation line, then reheat to $I$ if needed.

> [!TIP]+ Charts
> **Mollier** ($h$–$\omega$, $p_{atm}\approx1\,\text{bar}$): construction uses $h=c_{p,a}t+\omega(r_0+c_{p,v}t)$, $\varphi$-lines from $\omega(\varphi,p_s)$. At fixed $t$, $\partial h/\partial\omega=r_0+c_{p,v}t$; axes are drawn non-orthogonal so the useful region is readable. **Carrier diagram** is an alternative psychrometric chart.
