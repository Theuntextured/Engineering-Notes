# Kinds of Systems
### Closed System
- No mas transfers across its boundary
- $\dot{m}=0$
- Energy transfers are allowed
### Open System
- $\dot{m}\neq 0,\dot{E}\neq 0$
- Mass and energy can be transferred through the boundary
- To study this kind of system, we must refer to a control volume through which mass flows
### Isolated System
- $\dot{m}=0,\dot{E}=0$
- No mass nor energy transfers to surroundings

# System Descriptions
### Property
- Macroscopic feature of the system
- e.g. mass, volume, temperature etc.
- Can be assigned a numerical value with no knowledge of previous states
- Can be:
	- **Extensive:** ones which have numerical value proportional to quantity of matter within the system (mass, volume, potential and kinetic energy etc.)
	- **Intensive:** Numerical property independent of quantity of matter contained (e.g. pressure, temperature etc.)
	- **Specific:** Ratio between two extensive properties (e.g. specific volume $v=\frac{V}{m}$).
### State
- Condition in which the system is as described by its properties
- A subset of properties is sufficient to describe the system. The others can be determined with these.
- A **steady state** is one which has no property changing over time.
### Equilibrium
- **Mechanical POV:**
	- $\sum \vec{F}=\vec{0}$
- **Thermodynamics POV:**
	- Achieved if the following are satisfied:
		- Mechanical equilibrium
			- Pressure is the same at any point in the system
		- Thermal equilibrium
			- Temperature is the same through the whole system
		- Chemical equilibrium
			- Chemical composition does not change over time
		- Phase equilibrium
			- Uniform and invariable in chemical composition
### Process and Path
![[Pasted image 20260516161856.png]]

- A **process** is a transformation which the system changes its state and therefore also properties
- A **path** is the series of equilibrium states through which a system passes during a process
- A **thermodynamic cycle** is a sequence of a series of processes that begin and end at the same state
	- No net change of state occurs
- **Quasi-Equilibrium Process** is one in which the system is always infinitesimally close to equilibrium
	- Idealized process
	- The whole system can be described by using a numerical value for each property
	- Actual processes satisfy equilibrium only at beginning and end of process. A numerical value for each property is not sufficient.

# Energy
Can be:
- **Macroscopic:** energies a system has in respect to an outside reference frame
- **Microscopic:** Related to molecular activity and are therefore independent of any outside reference frame.

> [!TIP]+ Convention
> $Q>0$: heat is transferred **TO** the system
> $Q<0$: heat is REMOVED from the system

> [!NOTE] Heat is NOT a property! 
> It's a **boundary phenomenon**, since it does not depend on the state of a system. 
> It is therefore a path function.

> [!IMPORTANT]+
If no heat is transferred during a process, it is called **adiabatic**.

> [!SUMMARY]+ Work
> Just like heat, work is also not a property and is a path function.

$$
W=\int_{V_{1}}^{V_{2}}p\cdot dV
$$

We therefore MUST know $p$ as a function of $V$ to find $W$.

> [!WARNING]+ Idealized Process
> The above only works for ideal processes since in reality, $p$ would be a function of $V,x,y,z,t$.

# Efficiency and Cycles
$$
\begin{align}
\eta=\frac{W_{net}}{Q_{in}} && \eta=\frac{Q_{net}}{W_{in}} && \eta=\frac{\text{net output}}{\text{input}}
\end{align}
$$

### Power Cycles
_Direct Cycles_
- Heat input required $\implies$ Work/power is developed

$$
\eta=\frac{W_{cycle}}{Q_{in}}=\frac{{Q_{in}-|Q_{out}|/}}{Q_{in}}=1-\frac{|Q_{out}|}{Q_{in}}<1
$$

Refrigeration coefficient of performance:
$$
\beta=\frac{Q_{in}}{|W_{cycle}|}=\frac{Q_{in}}{|Q_{out}|-Q_{in}}
$$

Heat pump coefficient of performance:

$$
\gamma=\frac{|Q_{out}|}{|W_{cycle}|}=1+\beta
$$
