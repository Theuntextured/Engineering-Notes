> [!SUMMARY]+ Setup (turbine / compressor)
> Steady state, **adiabatic**, $\Delta E_K,\Delta E_P \approx 0$:
> $$\frac{\dot{W}_t}{\dot{m}}=h_{in}-h_{out}>0 \quad \text{(turbine)} \qquad \frac{\dot{W}_c}{\dot{m}}=h_{out}-h_{in}>0 \quad \text{(compressor, work input)}$$
> Pumps follow the same logic as compressors, but usually with liquid enthalpies and small specific volume changes.

Actual devices have **internal irreversibility** ⇒ $s_{out}>s_{in}$. Compare to an **isentropic** ($s_{out}=s_{in}$) process at the **same inlet state and exit pressure** → state $2s$ on Mollier / $h$–$s$ diagram.

## Turbine - maximum shaft work
2nd law (adiabatic CV): $\dot{\sigma}/\dot{m}=s_{out}-s_{in}\geq 0$

- $s_{out}<s_{in}$ impossible
- Reversible (isentropic) ⇒ $s_{out}=s_1$, $h_{out}=h_{2s}$ → **maximum** $\dot{W}_t/\dot{m}$

$$
\frac{\dot{W}_t}{\dot{m}}\bigg|_{\text{rev}}=h_1-h_{2s}
$$

$$
\boxed{\eta_{is,t}=\frac{(\dot{W}_t/\dot{m})_{\text{irr}}}{(\dot{W}_t/\dot{m})_{\text{rev}}}=\frac{h_1-h_2}{h_1-h_{2s}}<1}
$$

Ideal gas (constant $c_p$, same $p_{out}$, isentropic $T_{2s}$): $\eta_{is,t}=\dfrac{T_1-T_2}{T_1-T_{2s}}$

## Compressor - minimum shaft work
Same entropy argument ⇒ minimum work when $s_{out}=s_1$, $h_{out}=h_{2s}$:

$$
\frac{\dot{W}_c}{\dot{m}}\bigg|_{\text{rev}}=h_{2s}-h_1
$$

$$
\boxed{\eta_{is,c}=\frac{(\dot{W}_c/\dot{m})_{\text{rev}}}{(\dot{W}_c/\dot{m})_{\text{irr}}}=\frac{h_{2s}-h_1}{h_2-h_1}<1}
$$

Ideal gas (constant $c_p$): $\eta_{is,c}=\dfrac{T_{2s}-T_1}{T_2-T_1}$

> [!WARNING]+ Sign convention
> Turbine: actual work **smaller** than isentropic → ratio actual/rev **< 1**. Compressor: actual work **larger** → ratio rev/actual **< 1**.

## Reversible shaft work (general open system)
Steady, reversible, heat transfer with one reservoir, negligible ke/pe:

$$
\frac{\dot{Q}_{CV}}{\dot{m}}\bigg|_{\text{rev}}=\int_{1}^{2}T\,ds \qquad \frac{\dot{W}_{CV}}{\dot{m}}\bigg|_{\text{rev}}=-\int_{1}^{2}v\,dp
$$

From $\dot{\sigma}_{CV}=0$: $\dot{Q}_{CV}/\dot{m}=\int_1^2T\,ds$. Combine with the steady-flow 1st law and Gibbs relation to obtain $-\int v\,dp$.
