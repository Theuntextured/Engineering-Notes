> [!NOTE]+ Model assumptions
> Closed system, **ideal gas**, all steps **quasi-static / reversible**: two **isothermal** (at $T_H$, $T_C$) + two **adiabatic** expansions/compressions.

| Step | Process | Key relations |
|---|---|---|
| 1→2 | $T=T_H$ expansion | $Q_{IN}=Q_{12}=W_{12}=mRT_H\ln(V_2/V_1)$, $\Delta U_{12}=0$ |
| 2→3 | Adiabatic expansion | $Q=0$, $W_{23}=-\Delta U_{23}$, $\dfrac{T_3}{T_2}=\left(\dfrac{V_2}{V_3}\right)^{k-1}$ |
| 3→4 | $T=T_C$ compression | $Q_{OUT}=Q_{34}=mRT_C\ln(V_4/V_3)$, $\Delta U_{34}=0$ |
| 4→1 | Adiabatic compression | $Q=0$, $\dfrac{T_4}{T_1}=\left(\dfrac{V_1}{V_4}\right)^{k-1}$ |

Combining adiabatic relations gives $V_2/V_3=V_1/V_4$ and:

$$
\boxed{\eta_{\text{Carnot}}=1+\frac{Q_{OUT}}{Q_{IN}}=1-\frac{T_C}{T_H}<1}
$$

- Depends **only** on reservoir temperatures (Kelvin).
- $\eta \to 1$ only if $T_C \to 0\,\text{K}$.

## Link to Clausius inequality
Fully reversible Carnot power cycle between $T_1$ (hot) and $T_2$ (cold):

$$
\frac{Q_1}{T_1}+\frac{Q_2}{T_2}=0 \qquad \oint\frac{\delta Q_{\text{int,rev}}}{T}=0
$$

## Inverse Carnot (refrigeration / heat pump)
Same relation $Q_1/T_1+Q_2/T_2=0$ with $Q_1<0$ (heat rejected at hot side), $Q_2>0$ (heat absorbed at cold side):

$$
\boxed{\beta=\frac{Q_C}{W}=\frac{T_C}{T_H-T_C}} \qquad \boxed{\gamma=\frac{Q_H}{W}=\frac{T_H}{T_H-T_C}}
$$

($Q_C\equiv|Q_2|$, $Q_H\equiv|Q_1|$, $W=Q_H-Q_C$.)

## Carnot corollaries
> [!QUOTE]+ 1st corollary
> All **reversible** power cycles between the same two reservoirs have the **same** $\eta$.

> [!QUOTE]+ 2nd corollary
> An **irreversible** power cycle between the same reservoirs has **lower** $\eta$ than a reversible one:
> $$\eta_{\text{irr}}<\eta_{\text{rev}}$$

Same reservoirs ⇒ less heat from hot reservoir for same work: $Q_{in,\text{rev}}>Q_{in,\text{irr}}$.
