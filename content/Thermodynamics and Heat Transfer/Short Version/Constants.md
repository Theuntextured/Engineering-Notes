# Gas Constants

$$
\begin{align}
R & =8.314472\,J mol^{-1} K^{-1} \\
R_{air} & =287.05\,Jkg^{-1}K^{-1} \\
R_{steam} & =461.52Jkg^{-1}K^{-1} \\
\frac{R_{air}}{R_{water}} & =\frac{M_{water}}{M_{air}}\approx 0.622
\end{align}
$$
