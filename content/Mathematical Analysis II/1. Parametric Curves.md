> [!DEFINITION]+ Trace of a function
> Let $\gamma:[a,b]\to \mathbb{R}^n$
> The set
> 
> $$
> \gamma([a,b])\subset \mathbb{R}^n
> $$
> is called the **trace** of $\gamma$. (Image of $[a,b]$ through $\gamma$).

# Simple, Closed Curves and Continuity

**Let $\gamma \in C^0(I;\mathbb{R}^3)$**

We say that:

- $\gamma$ is **SIMPLE** if $\gamma(t_{1})\neq \gamma(t_{2})\,\forall t_{1},t_{2} \in [a,b)$. This means that $\gamma$ is injective in $[a,b)$, so it does not self-intersect.
- $\gamma$ is **CLOSED** of $\gamma(a)=\gamma(b)$

We say that $\gamma \in C^k(I;\mathbb{R}^n)$ if all of its components are of class $C^k$ in $I$.

---

# Regularity

Let $\gamma \in C^1(I;\mathbb{R}^3)$. $\forall t \in I$, the **tangent vector** to $\gamma$ at $\gamma(t)$ is:

$$
\gamma'(t)=(x'(t),y'(t),z'(t))
$$

We say that $\gamma$ is regular if:

$$
\gamma \in C^1(I;R^3) \text{ and } \gamma'(t)\neq(0,0,0)\, \forall t \in I
$$

$\gamma$ is pecewise regular if:

$$
I=[a,b] \text{ and } \exists a<t_{1}<t_{2}<\dots<t_{n}<b:\gamma \text{ is regular in } (a,t_{1}),(t_{1},t_{2})\dots(t_{n},b).
$$

# Length of a Curve

Let $\gamma:[a,b]\to \mathbb{R}^3$ be a piecewise regular function and $L(\gamma)$ be its length:

$$
L(\gamma)=\int_{a}^b ||\gamma'(t)||\,dt
$$
