
> [!DEFINITION]+
> $$
> \sum_{n=0}^\infty a_{n}(x-x_{0})^n
> $$

$$
\sum_{n=0}^\infty(x^n)=\begin{cases}
\frac{1}{1-x} &  & |x|<1 \\
+\infty &  & x\geq1 \\
\not \exists &  & x\leq-1
\end{cases}\implies\text{Convergence Set}=\{ x \in \mathbb{R}: |x|<1 \}
$$
## Root Criteria
#### Root Criteria 1
Let $(a_{n})_{n}\subset \mathbb{R}$ and let $R\in[0,+\infty]$ be the radius of convergence of the power series

$$
\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_{n}x^n
$$

Then,

$$
\lim_{ n \to \infty } \sqrt[n]{|a_{n}|}=l\in \mathbb{R}\implies R=\frac{1}{l}
$$

#### Root Criteria 2
*Same setup as above*

$$
\lim_{ n \to \infty } | \frac{a_{n+1}}{a_{n}}|=l\in \mathbb{R}\implies R=\frac{1}{l}
$$

---
Let $(a_{n})_{n},(b_{n})_{n}\subset \mathbb{R}$. Assume that:

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_{n}x^n,\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} b_{n}x^n
$$

Have radii of convergence $R_{a}\neq R_{b}$ respectively. Then,

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (a_{n}+b_{n})x^n \text{ has radius of convergence } R_{ab}=\min(R_{a},R_{b})
$$

> [!NOTE]+
> It is important to note that the two radii $R_{a},R_{b}$ are NOT equal!

## Analytical Functions

An analytical function at $x_{0}$ is one that is $C^\infty$ and can be represented as a Taylor series around $x_{0}$.

> [!THEOREM]+
> Let $I\subseteq \mathbb{R}$ be an interval and $f\in C^\infty(I)$. $f$ is analytic in $I$ if:
> 
> $$
> \exists C>0:|f^{(n)}(x)|\leq C^n\ \forall x \in I,\forall n \in \mathbb{N}
> $$
