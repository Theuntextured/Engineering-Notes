## Fundamentals of Measurement
* Measurements are never absolute truths; result dispersion indicates the presence of random factors.
* The goal is to determine if variations are caused by random chance or specific, real reasons.

| Factor Type | Characteristics | Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Systematic** | Big, regular, determinable causes. | Distorts data. |
| **Random** | Small, irregular, non-deterministic. | Confuses data (requires statistical treatment). |

## Uncertainty vs. True Value
> [!WARNING] The "True Value" Myth
> The concept of a defined "true value" (e.g., a gauge block being exactly 30.000 mm) is a simplification (VIM concept). Higher resolution instruments will almost always reveal differences.

* **Uncertainty Interval:** The range of values around a result where the measurand is reasonably expected to lie.
* A measurement's information is completely useless/incomplete without its corresponding uncertainty interval.

## Comparing Measurements
* **Overlapping Bands (Compatible):** You cannot state which result is higher. Differences could merely be random errors.
* **Separated Bands (Not Compatible):** You can definitively state the results are different.

## ISO 14253-1:2013 & Tolerances
> [!IMPORTANT] Guard-Banding Rule
> Because measurements have uncertainty, a result inside the limit might still physically be out of tolerance.

* **Manufacturer (Supplier):** Must add half the uncertainty range to the lower limit and remove it from the upper limit.
    * *Consequence:* Shrinks the acceptance zone $\rightarrow$ increases outbound extra costs/rejections.
* **Customer:** Must move half the uncertainty range under the lower limit and above the upper limit.
    * *Consequence:* Shrinks the rejection zone $\rightarrow$ increases inbound risk/costs of accepting bad parts.
* *Solution:* Adopting correct metrological techniques minimises uncertainty, thereby reducing costs and risks for everyone.

### Origins of Tolerances
* **Law or Contract:** Must strictly apply ISO 14253-1 uncertainty shifts.
* **Technological/Experimental Tests:** Limits found via physical pass/reject testing *already include* instrument uncertainty $\rightarrow$ no need to shift the limits further.

## Quality Standards & Management
* A measurement result depends on the entire complex: instrument, operator, environment, and measurand.
* **GUM (Guide to the expression of uncertainty in measurement):** Provides a practical, controlled scheme to evaluate uncertainty and avoid the costs of trial-and-error experience.

| Standard               | Scope                                             | Auditing Authority Capability                                                          |
| :--------------------- | :------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ISO 9001:2015**      | General Factory Certification.           | Cannot evaluate if the factory makes an acceptable product.                   |
| **ISO/IEC 17025:2005** | Lab Accreditation (Testing/Calibration). | Must be capable of performing the measurement better than the accredited lab. |
