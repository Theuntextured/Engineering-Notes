# Self-assessment Questions 1
1. Performance vs. properties: which manufacturing factors affect the mechanical
response of a tensile sample vs. a true structural part
> Grain size, mono/poly-crystalline structure, defects, temperature, pores, shape and direction of stress (depending on the material, it could have anisotropic responses to stress).
2. Performance vs. properties: compare the tensile strength of a material sample vs.
that extracted from a structural part?
> Statistically, a larger sample is more likely to contain defects that will lead to the failure of the material. Therefore, samples in tensile strength tests (which are small to reduce costs) will have greater strength than for example a cable-stayed bridge's steel cable, even if the material is the same.
3. Performance vs. properties: following questions n.1 and n.2, guess similar
answers assuming a complex geometry and large size of the structural part
> As stated, larger, more complex structures are more likely to fail under the same load. Both because of statistical distribution of defects, and because complex shapes will not have a uniform distribution of load. Some parts of the object will have greater local stress.
4. Why the dimensional scale of microstructure does affect the material properties?
> Smaller grains means greater total grain boundary area, which has internal friction which slows down dislocations. However, grain boundaries are the weakest part of a polycrystalline structure, and therefore will make the material more brittle, while having higher $\sigma_{y}$.

dimensional scales?
