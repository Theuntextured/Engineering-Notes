# Brigg's Law

$$
n\lambda=2d\sin\theta
$$
- $n\in \mathbb{Z}^+$
- $d$: spacing between lattice plates
- $\theta$: angle of incidence 

$$
d=\frac{a}{\sqrt{ h^2+k^2+l^2 }}
$$
# Arrhenius' Law

$$
\frac{N_{V}}{N_{tot}}=Ce^{-\frac{E_{V}}{kT}}
$$
- $N_{V}$ is the equilibrium number of vacancies, while $N_{tot}$ is the total number of atomic sites.

# Substitutional Alloy Rules
- $\Delta r<15\%$
- Same crystal structure (e.g. FCC, BCC etc.)
- Similar electronegativity - $\Delta E<0.4$ for partial solubility and $\Delta E<0.2$ for full solubility.
- A metal prefers to dissolve another metal of the same or higher valency
	- Same valency means any ratio of two can form, otherwise it will be limited

$$
# **Hall-Petch Law**

$$
\sigma_{y}=\sigma_{0}+\frac{k_{y}}{\sqrt{ d }}
$$
# Ramberg-Osgood Law
_In plastic field_

$$
\sigma=H\varepsilon^n
$$

# Secondary Creep

$$
\frac{d\varepsilon}{dt}=C\sigma^ne^{ -\frac{E_{CR}}{RT} }=A\sigma^n
$$
# Stress Intensity Factor
_Around Cracks, stress is higher than the nominal stress $\sigma_{0}$._

$$
\begin{align}
&K=\frac{\sigma_{m}}{\sigma_{0}}=2\sqrt{ \frac{r_{\text{major}}}{r_{\text{minor}}}}  \\
\implies & \sigma_{m}=2\sigma_{0}\sqrt{ \frac{r_{\text{major}}}{r_{\text{minor}}} }
\end{align}
$$
