> [!TIP]- Systems
> A system is a set of interacting components that:
> - Perform a function
> - Exchange information
> - Depend on each other to deliver a service
>   
>   In cybersecurity, a system includes:
>   - Hardware and software
>   - Networks and data
>   - Users and operators
>   - Procedures and dependencies

> [!CHECK] IT vs OT
> **IT**: Information technology - systems manage data, computation and communication
> **OT:** Operational technology - systems control physical processes.
> 
> Modern infrastructures combine IT and OT, creating new dependancies and new attack paths.

## Definitions of Cybersecurity
### Operational
*Cybersecurity is how individuals and organizations reduce the risks of cyberattacks, to protect devices, services we access from theft and damage, and to protect unauthorized access to information.*

#### System-Level
*A set of activities necessary to:
- Protect networks, information systems, computer devices and electronic communications from cyber threats
- Ensure their confidentiality, integrity and availability, also ensuring their resilience*

### National-Scale
*Practice that allows an entity to protect:*
- Its physical assets
- Confidentiality, integrity and availability of its information from threats from the [[#Cyberspace]].

---
### Core Definition
All the above scopes link to a shared core:
- Confidentiality, integrity, availability (*CIA*)
- Resilience
- Risk reduction
---
## Cyberspace

> [!TIP]+ Definition
> Cyberspace is the complex environment resulting from the interaction of people, software, services, and networks connected to the Internet. It is:
> - Dynamic and evolving
> - Shaping the human behaviour and automation
> - Governed by technical, legal and social rules.

Cyberspace can be considered one of the most complex things that mankind has ever built. It has several levels:
- Human interaction
- Cloud/Services
- Applications
- Middleware
- Operating System
- Firmware
- Hardware

Because of this complexity and dependencies, vulnerabilities can appear.
