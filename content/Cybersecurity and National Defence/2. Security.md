
> [!TIP] Definition
> Security is the state of being free from dangers or threats.

> [!WARNING] Danger
> Danger is the intrinsic property of quality accountable for a particular factor having the potential to generate a harmful event. It is present even in the absence of malicious actors.

> [!ERROR] Threat
> A threat is a statement of intention to inflict pain, injury, damage or other hostile action in retribution for something done or not done. Unlike danger, it is intentional and involves a hostile agent.

---
### Assets

> [!CHECK]- Assets
> - Tangible (e.g., a physical item such as hardware, computing platform, network device, technology component) 
> - Intangible (e.g., data, information, software, trademark, copyright, intellectual property, image, reputation).
> 	- Often the primary target of cyberattacks

Loss of assets has an impact which should be evaluated considering:
- Value
- Criticality
- Irreplaceability
- Mission role

---
### Risk

> [!TIP]- Definitions
> Risk has several definitions:
> - *Combination of the probability of occurrence of an injury and the severity of that injury.*
> - The possibility that human actions or events lead to consequences that have an impact on what humans value.
> - Probability (P) of reaching the potential level of Harm (S) under the conditions of use or exposure to a given factor or agent or their combination.
> - Set of the possibility of an event and its consequences on objectives.
> - Combination of the probability and consequence of the occurrence of a specific hazardous event.

> [!BRIEF]
> Risk $R=S\times P$
> - $P$ = Probability of occurrence
>  - $S$ = Severity of the impact

> [!INFO]- NIST SP 800-30

Risk-based security management involves:
1. Prepare a detailed asset inventory
2. For each asset:
	1. Analyse the possible threats
	2. Prepare the appropriate protections and countermeasures
	3. Evaluate the residual risks

Insurance tends to cover the residual cyber risks.
