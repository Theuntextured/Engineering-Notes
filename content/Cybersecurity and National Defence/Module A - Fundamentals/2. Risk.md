
> [!IMPORTANT] Impact of Asset Loss
> Losing assets has an impact which depends on several factors:
> - Value
> - Criticality
> - Irreplaceability
> - Mission Role

Risk $R$ takes into account both the impact (severity $S$) of a danger, and its probability $P$.

> [!ABSTRACT] Risk Formula
> $$
> R=S\times P
> $$

> [!BUG] Insurance
> Insurance pays out on RESIDUAL risk in case of damage, not lack of control. Also, if the attack is by a state-related entity, it is considered an act of war and is therefore not reimbursed.

National agencies provide standardized risk assessment and treatment methods. For example, Italy has the [Agency for Digital Italy](https://www.agid.gov.it/it), or the [National Cybersecurity Authority (ACN)](https://www.acn.gov.it/portale/en/home).

> [!CHECK] Italian Cyber Risk Management Tool - ISO 31000
> A tool to define, document, monitor and act upon cyber-risks within an organization.
