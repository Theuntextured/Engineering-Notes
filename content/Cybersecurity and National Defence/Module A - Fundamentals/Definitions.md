

| **Term**                            | **Definition**                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| System                              | A set of interacting components that perform a function, exchange information and depend on each other to deliver a service. This includes hardware, software, networks, data, users, operators, procedures and dependencies. |
| IT (Information Technology) Systems | Systems managing data, computation and communications                                                                                                                                                                         |
| OT (Operational Technology) Systems | Systems controling physical processes (Such as industral controllers, sensors, motors etc.)                                                                                                                                   |
| Cybersecurity (Operational Side)    | How individuals and organizations reduce the risk of cyber-attacks, protecting devices and services from damage or data leaks.                                                                                                |
| Cybersecurity (System Level)        | Ensuring the protection of networks, information systems, computer services and electronic communications from cyber threats, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, availability (*CIA*) and their resilience.                 |
| Cybersecurity (National-Scale)      | Practice that allows an entity to protect its physical assets, confidentiality, integrity and availability of its information from threats that come from the cyberspace.                                                     |
| Cyberspace                          | The complex environment resulting from the interaction of people, software, services and networks connected to the internet. It is divided into physical, logical and cyber-persona layers.                                   |
| CIA (Or RID in Italian)             | Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability (Riservatezza, Integrità, Disponibilità)                                                                                                                                             |
| Big Data                            | Massive, heterogeneous data streams collected continuously.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Datacracy                           | Decisions and power increasingly depend on data access and analytics.                                                                                                                                                         |
| Data-driven Society                 | Services, markets and institutions optimize using data and models.                                                                                                                                                            |
| Non-Repudiation                     | The property that provides protection against an entity falsely denying having performed a particular action.                                                                                                                 |
