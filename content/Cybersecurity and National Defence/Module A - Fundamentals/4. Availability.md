
> [!TIP]- Definition
> Ensuring timely and reliable access to and use of information.

Threats can be Dos/DDoS attacks (overloading servers/clients with requests), ransomware outages, physical disruptions etc.

The probability $A(t)$ that the system will function correctly at a given instant $t$ is affected by metrics such as:
- Uptime
- Mean time between failures (MTBF)
- Mean time to repair (MTTR)
Many services publish availability guarantees in their service level agreements.
